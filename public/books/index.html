The Lighthouse in Me

A Journey of Self-Discovery, Inner Healing and Awakening

The light you have been searching for has been within you all along.

In 1986, a tragedy in Durban changed Reshma's family for ever. For years she carried it quietly, building a career and a family and keeping the grief where no one could see it. This is the story of what happened when she finally turned to face it, and of the slow, honest work of healing.
