About me

From the boardroom to the page

“Your story holds more than what has happened to you.”

Reshma Subramoney is an award-winning motivational speaker, author and philanthropist from South Africa.

Her own journey through grief and healing became the foundation for a life spent helping others find their way back to the light within themselves. In her memoir, The Lighthouse in Me, she writes openly about the 1986 tragedy that shaped her family, the years she carried it in silence, and the slow work of healing.

Before she became an author, Reshma built her career as a senior executive at Discovery and Hollard, working with Discovery's businesses in the United Kingdom and the United States. At Discovery she led Discovery Vitality and PruProtect to successful ISO certification.

She speaks to corporate audiences, community groups and anyone ready to face their own story. She also runs WOW Projects Academy.

The Lighthouse in Me

Some of us spend so long holding everything together that we forget to ask how we really are.

In 1986, a tragedy in Durban changed Reshma's family for ever. For years she carried it quietly, building a career and a family and keeping the grief where no one could see it. This is the story of what happened when she finally turned to face it.

The book is written for anyone who has survived something and kept going without ever stopping to feel it. It does not promise that the pain disappears. It shows that there is light to be found anyway.
