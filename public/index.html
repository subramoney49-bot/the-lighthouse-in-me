About the book

A story of loss, and the long way back to the light

Some of us spend so long holding everything together that we forget to ask how we really are.

In 1986, a tragedy in Durban changed Reshma Subramoney's family for ever. For years she carried it quietly, building a career and a family, and keeping the grief where no one could see it.

The Lighthouse in Me is the story of what happened when she finally turned to face it. It moves through her childhood, the people who held her up, the move from Durban to Johannesburg, her years in corporate South Africa, and the slow, honest work of healing.

The book is written for anyone who has survived something and kept going without ever quite stopping to feel it. It does not promise that the pain disappears. It shows that there is light to be found anyway.

About the author

Reshma Subramoney

Reshma Subramoney is an award-winning motivational speaker, author and philanthropist based in South Africa. Her own journey through grief and healing became the foundation for a life spent helping others find their way back to the light within themselves.

Before she wrote her story, Reshma built a career as a senior executive at Discovery and Hollard, working with Discovery's businesses in the United Kingdom and the United States. She is a Lean Six Sigma Master Black Belt and a qualified counsellor and trainer. She has delivered keynotes at the Customer Experience Africa Summit and runs WOW Projects Academy.
