From the archive

The 1986 newspaper reports

In June 1986, eleven members of Reshma's family in Chatsworth became seriously ill after food was cooked with what they believed was peanut oil. It was a stolen veterinary vitamin D concentrate. Reshma was eight years old. Her grandmother, her mother, her sister Lekha and the family's domestic worker, Cynthia Madlala, died. These are the newspaper reports from that winter, kept by the family for forty years and retold in The Lighthouse in Me.
